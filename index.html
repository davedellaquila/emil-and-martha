About Emil and Martha

E & M Photographs from the family albums will be added here.

Emil

Emil Dell'Aquila was an early employee at HP and at Fairchild Semiconductor, where he worked in international sales — part of the generation that carried Silicon Valley out into the world. As a young man he served in the military. He was a photographer, and a classic-car man, and — his family will tell you — a very handsome one. He passed away on July 1, 2026, and is missed every day.

Martha

Martha Dell'Aquila came to this country from Central America, dark-haired and far from home, and built a life and a family here. Her Honda Accord stayed in the family for years after her — some cars, like some people, are simply part of the household. She is remembered with love by everyone who knew her.

This is a beginning, not a biography. The fuller picture of Emil and Martha lives in the memories of the people below — please add yours.

Share a Story

If you knew Emil or Martha — a memory, a photograph, a moment that has stayed with you — we would love for you to share it here. There is no account to create and no form to fill in. It works by email:

  1. Send an email to dave@dellaquila.com with the subject line Emil and Martha.
  2. Write your story in the body of the email, just as you would tell it.
  3. Attach any photographs you would like to include.

What you write becomes your article on this page, published under your name with your email address shown, exactly as you sent it. Stories appear automatically — this page belongs to everyone who remembers them.
